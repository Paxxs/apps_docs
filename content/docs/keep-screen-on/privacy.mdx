# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how KeepScreenOn handles your data and protects your privacy. We are committed to ensuring the privacy and security of our users.

## Data Collection

**KeepScreenOn does not collect any personal data.** The app has no analytics, no tracking, and no user accounts. It does not transmit your content, your History, or any usage information to us or to any third party.

## Your Content

Everything you share or pick stays on your device:

- Shared and picked content is stored locally on your device and listed in your History
- Your History and its stored files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server
- Deleting a History item also removes its stored files from your device

The only exception is **web pages**. When you view a web Shared Item, the app loads the live page directly from that website, exactly as a browser would. The app itself stores only the page's URL, not a copy of the page.

## Network Access

KeepScreenOn accesses the network only to load the live web pages you choose to view. It makes no other network connections. Photos, PDFs, documents, and other files are previewed entirely on-device and are never sent over the network.

## Permissions

KeepScreenOn requests only the permissions it needs to preview your content:

- **Photo Library Access**: To let you pick images from your library for viewing

The app keeps the screen awake only while you are viewing a preview; normal auto-lock is restored when you leave.

Notably, KeepScreenOn **does not request or require**:

- Location services
- Contacts
- Microphone or camera
- Background processing beyond viewing your content
- Any other personal data

## Third-Party Services

KeepScreenOn does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

## Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this privacy policy will be reflected in the application and on this page.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support channels provided in the App Store.
